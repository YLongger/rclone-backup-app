Personal Backup (rclone)

This is a personal, non-commercial setup. The owner uses the open-source tool rclone to back up their own files to their own Google Drive.

It is not offered to the public and has no other users. It only accesses the Google Drive of the account that signs in, and only to upload and verify the owner's backups.

這是個人非商業用途：擁有者用開源工具 rclone，把自己的檔案備份到自己的 Google 雲端硬碟。不對外提供服務，只存取登入者本人的雲端硬碟。
